# Ilustración de Syncrash

`icon-source.png` también se generó con Imagegen el 23/09/2026. `syncrash.ico` empaqueta esa imagen con transparencia en tamaños de 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 y 256 píxeles, mediante `Build-Icon.ps1`. `mark.png` es la versión de 64 píxeles usada en la cabecera.

Prompt del icono:

> Create a professional Windows app icon for Syncrash, an independent stability patch for the Roman strategy game Imperivm. Square 1024x1024 image with genuinely transparent background. A single compact emblem: a crisp, bold ivory capital S integrated into an angular Roman scutum shield, deep oxblood red enamel shield face, restrained aged bronze outline. Flat vector-like precision with only very subtle material shading, sober premium software brand, strong silhouette, readable at 16 and 32 pixels. Centered, shield occupies 82% of square, generous transparent margin, no surrounding square tile, no scene, no words, no tiny details, no laurels, no swords, no glow, no drop shadow, no photorealism. Symmetric outer shield and simple S negative-space mark. This is the final app icon, not a mockup or a presentation sheet.

`banner.png` es una ilustración original generada con la herramienta integrada Imagegen el 23/09/2026. No se ha extraído del juego ni de Community Mod. Solo se utiliza como fondo de la pantalla; todos los textos y controles son nativos de Windows.

> Create a wide cinematic painterly banner for Syncrash, an independent Windows patch utility for the historical Roman RTS Imperivm. Original historical-scene illustration, no copied game art.
